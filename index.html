01 — About
A real player, not a perfect one

I'm Orly. I don't win every game. I study my losses, fix one mistake at a time, and come back stronger. That's what chess taught me about life.

WHAT I DO

Think before I move

Years of playing trained me to slow down and calculate:

  • Look at the whole board first
  • Find 2-3 good moves, pick the best
  • Stay patient when position is hard
HOW I PLAY

Solid, bold, honest

My style in three words:

  • Solid openings — no cheap tricks
  • Bold middlegame — I fight for center
  • Calm endgame — technique wins
